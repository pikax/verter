# B4 — Logical source units, mapping composition, and atomic publication

**Status:** PROPOSED amendment / LOCKED. **Predecessors:** B2 and B3.

B4 owns logical source units and stable source identities, fragment placement,
source-space and assembly mapping composition, and atomic compiler-artifact
publication. It implements the ratified `fragment-assembly.md` contract.

B4 must evaluate the emitter/mapping disposition ledger and choose representations
from evidence. This charter does not preselect `CodeTransform` or any current emitter
by name. Every current owner is Preserved, Converged, Replaced, Deleted, or Deferred
exactly as accepted; displaced owners are deleted in the owning cutover.

B4 does not implement Vue/Svelte semantics, helper choices, runtime topology, or
backend corrections. Exit proves fragment and final parse contracts, source-space
composition, exact product sets, typed all-or-nothing refusal, and absence of
generated-product reparsing.
