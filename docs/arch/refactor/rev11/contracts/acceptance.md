# Acceptance contract

Acceptance is an immutable receipt bound to node/charter digest, the admission-start lease, immutable dispatch packet, frozen candidate finalization, base, candidate, integration and tree identities; exact predecessor receipt digests; gate and review evidence; external authorizations; and final-tree equivalence. The receipt's base, candidate, and changed paths must exactly equal the finalized base delta. Here final-tree equivalence means `integration_tree` is the real tree of `integration_sha`, the candidate is its ancestor, and every base→candidate changed path/blob survives unchanged. It deliberately does not require `candidate_tree == integration_tree`: unrelated conflict-free integration advances are permitted and receive a separate touched-domain integration gate. Malformed, unknown, stale, forged, partial, skipped, or contradictory evidence refuses acceptance. Generated state and mutable READY declarations have no authority. Historical receipts are accepted only through the explicit legacy schema and exact pinned identities.

External requirements do not freeze the admission-start SHA. READY, admission, and dispatch require the exact immutable static directive slot only. A directive-mode runtime authorization is created—not caller-imported—after `candidate-finalize`, by projecting the exact finalized candidate and authority identity from that receipt plus the static slot. Gate/review evidence and acceptance refuse a missing, earlier, or different candidate-bound authorization.
