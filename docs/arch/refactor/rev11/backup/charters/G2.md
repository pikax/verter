# G2 — FlightCell-owned same-key production

**Status:** LOCKED — contains binding acceptance criteria from the ratified timing directive; this charter is not separately authorized or dispatched. See the [landed timing ruling](../rulings/ARCHITECT-RULING-2026-08-23-TIMING-ARCHITECTURE.md).  
**Class:** Foundational.  
**Predecessor:** `G1`.

## Objective

Make `FlightCell` the one reusable production owner for same-key, exact-basis work, independent of whichever request first demanded it.

## Acceptance criteria

- One useful producer flight exists per key and exact basis.
- Producer lifetime is independent of any request handler.
- Multiple waiters join the same producer.
- Foreground demand can promote an existing background flight.
- Cancelling one waiter does not cancel production still useful to other waiters.
- Each waiter has independent cancellation and an independent absolute deadline.
- The absolute deadline covers queue admission, execution, and response; no second timeout begins after admission.
- Completion publishes durable `ReadyAt { basis, artifact/read-set stamp }` state.
- Waiters subscribe before rechecking durable state.
- Obsolete results are rejected before publication.
- Panic, shutdown, cancellation, failure, budget exhaustion, and finalization resolve every waiter exactly once and publish no false complete value.

## Mandatory migrations and deletion ownership

`FlightCell` is exact-basis semantic production: one producer per key and exact basis, joined waiters, independent cancellation and deadlines, and durable `ReadyAt`. It is not a cross-basis lifecycle sequencer and does not join semantic flights across bases. Cross-snapshot semantic-flight joining remains disabled by default (`architecture.md` §10.3). G2 does not grow a pending-rerun, latest-generation runner, or heterogeneous open/close/reopen primitive to make those cells look like `FlightCell`.

G2 migrates reusable exact-basis production from the listed cells instead of extending each local implementation. It does not absorb heterogeneous lifecycle serialization or cross-basis coalescing. Those remain on `ProviderHub` under H2; H3 owns when LSP demand invokes them. None of these cells survives as a second generic coordinator.

**Exact-basis production G2 absorbs and deletes:**

- `ImportSyncMemo::{locks,in_flight}` / `InFlightPublication`;
- `external_ts_sync::{QueryDedupeRegistry, QueryAdmission, InflightSlot}` (the module-local slot, not `cache_runtime::InflightSlot`) and its exact-key join/shared-cancellation callers. G2 maps that query identity onto G1/F2 `QueryIdentity` plus exact basis and delivers durable `ReadyAt` to joiners; leader-only elision without result delivery is not the surviving contract;
- `CarrierPublicationStore::{lanes, PublicationLane}` leader/waiter join per `FrameworkArtifactId`;
- `MetaRuntimeImpl.pendingEngines` (component-meta JS bootstrap join) when that bootstrap is host-owned;
- the unjoined producer portion of hover provenance when H3 migrates that capability instead of deleting it.

**Mixed cells — G2 absorbs only exact-basis waiter-join production; H2 owns serialized provider lifecycle and cross-basis protocol coalescing:**

- `VerterLanguageServer::ide_sync_repair_locks`, `IdeSyncRepairLease`, `IdeSyncRepairLane`, `ide_sync_open_generations`, `ide_sync_next_generation`, and their begin/current-or-init/is-open/close generation helpers. The map, lease, lane, generation, and retirement state are one H2 `ProviderHub` lifecycle cutover: open, close, reopen, repair, forced provider reopen, and document-generation ABA. They are not G2 waiter declarations and G2 does not delete them. G2 supplies only the exact-basis production flight a repair or demand joins. H3 owns when LSP demand invokes that lifecycle.
- `resync_singleflight::ResyncCoordinator`. Folding arbitrarily many triggers into one pending rerun, serializing cross-basis sweeps, and returning immediately from followers are H2 cross-basis coalescing, not `FlightCell` joining. G2 supplies any exact-basis production a resync demand waits on.
- `TsserverCarrierRefresh::{requested_generation,urgent_generation,running,completion,failure}` plus `schedule_carrier_refresh`/`wait_for_carrier_refresh`. One runner that advances toward the latest requested generation and promotes urgency across generations is H2 protocol coalescing. G2 supplies waiters for the exact applied basis. H2 preserves the semantic `applied_generation`.
- `CarrierSyncState` / `PendingSubmission` / `PendingKind` and `TsgoSharedProvider::sync`. Per-carrier latest-pending coalescing (inject and close share one slot; newest wins; bursts collapse to one barrier) is the shared-tsgo sibling of `TsserverCarrierRefresh`, not `FlightCell` joining. G2 supplies waiters for the exact applied/synced basis. H2 preserves barrier-synced `CarrierSlot` content.
- `DeclOverlayOwner::{slots,locks,root_reconcile_epochs}` per-declaration-path open/close serialization, generation ABA, and `close_pending`. That is serialized overlay lifecycle, not `FlightCell` joining. G2 supplies only exact-basis production waiters. H2 converges the independent serialization into `ProviderHub`; the reachability graph is preserved overlay-membership domain state, not a second generic coordinator.
- `LazyTransport` / `LazyOverlayCore::{ensure, carrier_gates}` drop-lock establishment singleflight and per-carrier inject/retract gates. Concurrent demands join one provider-transport establishment; same-path inject/retract serialize on `carrier_gates`. That is H2 attach/establishment lifecycle, not `FlightCell` joining. The 15/20/2-second shared-overlay family remains the binding duration fence and is not a G2 deletion.
- `ProjectTsserverProvider::providers` keyed `OnceCell` collapsing concurrent cold spawns, and `LazyManagedTypeProvider` activation `OnceCell` plus mutex/cooldown. Those are H2 attach/establishment, not `FlightCell` joining. Editor-side plugin refresh and Native Preview relay joins are TCM4, because `ProviderHub` is LSP-only.
- `TsserverTransport::membership_recovery` and `MEMBERSHIP_RECOVERY_COOLDOWN`. Local cooldown and cross-basis coalescing are H2 lifecycle. G2 supplies only an exact-basis production flight if membership recovery produces one.

H2's lifecycle and coalescing owner is existing `ProviderHub` ownership (provider selection, lifecycle, epochs, synchronization, cancellation, and deadlines). It is not a second `FlightCell` and not a local duplicate flight cell.

The mixed-cell and exact-basis lists are the H2/G2 rows of the same-key coalescer inventory in `charters/K3.md`. That inventory is a NAMED list, built by documented search over the live tree rather than by recall; re-running that search is not a close condition for any block (`rulings/MAINTAINER-RULING-COALESCER-CLOSURE-IS-NAMED-DISPOSITION.md`). A newly discovered same-key coalescer is classified there before it can be treated as out of G2's list.

G2 is the primary deletion owner for displaced exact-basis flight fields, helpers, and modules as each migrates, including `ImportSyncMemo` flight mechanics, `external_ts_sync::{QueryDedupeRegistry, QueryAdmission, InflightSlot}`, `CarrierPublicationStore::{lanes, PublicationLane}`, and `MetaRuntimeImpl.pendingEngines`. H2 is the primary deletion owner for the displaced lifecycle and coalescing coordinators (`ide_sync_repair_locks`, `IdeSyncRepairLease`, `IdeSyncRepairLane` serialization and generation ABA helpers, `ResyncCoordinator`, tsserver carrier-refresh runner state, shared-tsgo `CarrierSyncState`/`PendingSubmission`, `DeclOverlayOwner` independent path-lock/`close_pending` mutex, `LazyTransport`/`LazyOverlayCore` establishment singleflight, `ProjectTsserverProvider::providers` keyed `OnceCell`, `LazyManagedTypeProvider` activation, membership-recovery cooldown) once `ProviderHub` lifecycle replaces them. H3 owns foreground/background LSP settlement and hover-provenance policy. K3 verifies the full residual closure and deletes any missed residue before K3 can close.

## Proof

Forced interleaving tests prove one producer, join, foreground promotion, subscribe-before-recheck, independent waiter cancellation/deadlines, deadline-before-admission on a full queue, durable completion, and stale-publication rejection. Correctness tests use exact handshakes and an outer watchdog, never yields, polling, or elapsed thresholds.
