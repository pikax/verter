# G3 — Bounded CPU execution and owner-affine commands

**Status:** LOCKED — contains binding acceptance criteria from the ratified timing directive; this charter is not separately authorized or dispatched. See the [landed timing ruling](../rulings/ARCHITECT-RULING-2026-08-23-TIMING-ARCHITECTURE.md).  
**Class:** Foundational.  
**Predecessors:** `B2`, `G2`.

## Objective

Provide bounded, priority-aware execution that protects foreground demand and schedules only prerequisite-ready work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every queue and fork family has an explicit bound.
- Foreground/interactive work has reserved capacity and priority-aware admission.
- Background work uses latest-wins coalescing and explicit supersession.
- Work retained or started during edit churn is proportional to active keys/documents, not edit count.
- Foreground demand promotes the exact G2 flight rather than waiting for a background quiet window.
- Global-handler-idle or “server quiet for N ms” is neither readiness nor admission authority.
- G3 may provide a reusable bounded-admission primitive whose reservation a caller can race against cancellation and an absolute deadline. G3 does not own, accept, or cut over any provider-request path. Racing `verter_tsgo_api::actor::ClientHandle::request` bounded-channel reservation against cancellation and one absolute deadline is one indivisible provider-request operation and belongs solely to H2. H2 may consume the G3 primitive; G3 does not implement that call. The same single-owner split applies to TCM2: mapper-process JSON-RPC admission, cancellation, and deadline belong solely to TCM2. TCM2 may consume the G3 primitive; G3 does not implement, accept, or delete the `@verter/typescript-content-mapper` protocol path.

## Transitional ownership

G3 provides the replacement admission semantics for `handler_guard::{wait_for_handlers_idle,wait_for_handlers_quiet,wait_for_idle_counter,wait_for_quiet_counter}` and their `workspace_scanner` call sites, and for `TsserverTransport::{wait_for_interactive_idle,request_background_batch_results_with_preemption}`. G5 is the deletion owner for the handler-global-idle and tsserver-interactive-idle scheduling bridges. G3 does not cut over `verter_tsgo_api::actor::ClientHandle::request`; H2 is the sole acceptance and deletion owner of that provider-request admission, cancellation, and deadline. G3 does not cut over mapper-process JSON-RPC admission; TCM2 is the sole owner of that path. K3 verifies no host/LSP/provider caller or facade retains any old scheduling role and deletes missed residue before closing.

## Proof

Tests prove bounded queue depth, reserved foreground admission under background saturation, latest-wins work counts, and explicit supersession. A reusable bounded-admission primitive, if shipped, proves reservation can complete, cancel, or hit a deadline before a slot is available; those tests do not accept the tsgo `ClientHandle::request` path. Full-queue cancellation and deadline of that provider call are H2 proofs. No proof samples handler counters until they look idle.
