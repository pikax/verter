# H2 — Project-scoped ProviderHub bindings

**Status:** LOCKED — contains binding acceptance criteria from the ratified timing directive; this charter is not separately authorized or dispatched. See the [landed timing ruling](../rulings/ARCHITECT-RULING-2026-08-23-TIMING-ARCHITECTURE.md).  
**Class:** Foundational.  
**Predecessors:** `G5`, `H1`.

## Objective

Make provider readiness causal and exact while keeping provider selection, lifecycle, capabilities, and epochs project-scoped.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every readiness-sensitive provider transition mints an exact applied-generation or “Program ready for basis” receipt.
- Queue admission is inside cancellation and the one absolute deadline; admission, execution, and response share that deadline.
- H2 is the sole acceptance and cutover owner of the provider-request admission, cancellation, and deadline. `verter_tsgo_api::actor::ClientHandle::request` races bounded-channel reservation/admission against cancellation and that absolute deadline; `Actor::serve_one` consumes the same absolute deadline instead of starting a duration after dequeue. G3 may supply a reusable bounded-admission primitive that this call consumes; G3 does not implement, accept, or delete this path.
- H2 owns serialized provider lifecycle and cross-basis protocol coalescing on `ProviderHub`: open/close/reopen/repair/forced reopen including document-generation ABA (`VerterLanguageServer::ide_sync_repair_locks`, `IdeSyncRepairLease`, `IdeSyncRepairLane`, and generation helpers — one mixed cutover; H2 deletes every one of those declarations); folding many resync triggers into one pending rerun (`ResyncCoordinator`); one tsserver carrier-refresh runner that advances toward the latest requested generation and promotes urgency across generations (`TsserverCarrierRefresh`); the shared-tsgo per-carrier latest-pending coalescer (`CarrierSyncState` / `PendingSubmission` / `PendingKind` on `TsgoSharedProvider::sync` — inject and close share one slot, newest wins); declaration-overlay open/close serialization (`DeclOverlayOwner` path locks and `close_pending`; `root_reconcile_epochs` high-water is preserved stale-pass authority under `ProviderHub`); one-in-flight shared-overlay establishment (`LazyTransport` / `LazyOverlayCore::{ensure, carrier_gates}`); keyed one-spawn `ProjectTsserverProvider::providers` `OnceCell`; `LazyManagedTypeProvider` activation `OnceCell`/mutex/cooldown; the cold-start first-`updateSnapshot` validation join in BOTH `verter_tsgo_api` clients (`TsgoClient::{first_snapshot_validated, first_validation_lock}` at `client.rs:48,52` and the structurally identical `ApiAttachClient::{first_snapshot_validated, first_validation_lock}` at `api_attach.rs:42,46` (the two field declarations are byte-identical; the join bodies differ) — one caller runs the wire rail while concurrent clones sharing the engine block, then take the atomic fast path); the per-configured-project bootstrap RESERVATION (`TsserverTypeProvider::project_bootstraps`, `verter_type_runtime` `tsserver/ipc.rs:1873`, reserved at `:2610-2618`) where one caller performs the contentless companion bootstrap that instantiates the project and invokes the plugin's `getExternalFiles` while every other source for that project returns early at `:2628-2630` relying on the winner; and membership-recovery cooldown coalescing. Editor-side `typescriptPluginRefreshScheduler` and Native Preview `transition` are TCM4, because `ProviderHub` is LSP-only. Those are not G2 `FlightCell` semantics. G2 supplies only exact-basis production waiters those lifecycle operations join. The 15/20/2-second shared-overlay family remains the binding duration fence on the establishment cell; H2 does not retarget those durations by deleting or absorbing `LazyTransport`.
- The shared-overlay 20-second fallback bound (`verter_lsp` shared attach/overlay/close 15/20/2-second family, overlay slot) is an unsettled serving-policy duration, not a watchdog and not a transitional mechanism. H2 must not reclassify, retarget, shorten, or delete it without a separate architecture ruling and exact timer tests.
- The 2 ms atomic polling loop in `verter_tsgo_api::actor::wait_cancelled` is replaced by an exact event-driven cancellation witness.
- Foreground requests await or promote the exact prerequisite through G2.
- Provider transport completion is never inferred from sleep, handler idle, interactive idle, or pseudo-idle.
- The completion recovery loop in `server/nav_features.rs` using `[50, 150, 300]` millisecond sleeps is deleted.
- At most one retry is permitted after an exact provider-applied receipt where the provider protocol genuinely requires reopening.
- Provider races and silent semantic fallback remain forbidden.

## Exact recovery cutovers

- `provider_query_with_bounded_recovery` currently accepts a receipt-less `resync: FnOnce() -> Future<Output = ()>` and is called by hover, definition, and type-definition paths. H2 changes that contract to return exact applied-receipt authorization for the requested basis. A retry is authorized only by that receipt, remains identity-fenced, and occurs at most once when the protocol requires reopening. H2 deletes the old `Future<Output = ()>` bridge and cuts every old receipt-less call form over to the exact contract.
- Tsserver membership recovery currently uses `recover_companion_membership`, a local `membership_recovery` cooldown stamp, `MEMBERSHIP_RECOVERY_COOLDOWN`, and `recovery_attempts < 2` plus `yield_now` loops in `get_hover` and `get_diagnostics`. H2 deletes the local cooldown/coalescing coordinator and the time/yield retry loops, and replaces them with `ProviderHub` lifecycle plus an exact applied-generation/Program-ready receipt followed by at most one protocol-required retry. G2 supplies only an exact-basis production flight if that recovery produces one.

## Global readiness replacement

`VerterReady`/`VerterReadyParams` (`$/verter/ready`) and `TypeProviderSyncComplete`/`TypeProviderSyncCompleteParams` (`$/verter/typeProviderSyncComplete`) are not final provider readiness. Their shared `gen` field currently means incompatible things: an initialization generation in `background_init` and `notify_editor_carrier_store_changed`, but a per-document content-transition generation in `SyncCoordinator`. Equality of those numbers cannot authorize a provider query. H2 supplies the exact provider-applied, project/document/dependency basis that H3 exposes to each demanded route; no global notification or generation latch substitutes for it.

## Migration and deletion ownership

H2 supplies provider policy, receipts, serialized lifecycle, and cross-basis protocol coalescing. G2 absorbs and deletes only reusable exact-basis flight mechanics those operations join. H2 preserves and converges the already-causal `ProviderReadyReceipt`, `CarrierCommitStamp`, semantic applied-generation state, barrier-synced `CarrierSlot` content, and the `DeclOverlayOwner` overlay-membership domain state under `ProviderHub` (reaching roots, per-root pass stamps, and `root_reconcile_epochs` stale-pass high-water). H2 is the primary deletion owner for: the completion backoff; receipt-less `provider_query_with_bounded_recovery` resync contract/callers; membership cooldown/coalescing and time/yield retry loops; the entire IDE-repair mixed cutover (`ide_sync_repair_locks`, `IdeSyncRepairLease`, `IdeSyncRepairLane` lifecycle serialization, and generation ABA helpers); `ResyncCoordinator`; `TsserverCarrierRefresh` runner/urgency state; shared-tsgo `CarrierSyncState`/`PendingSubmission` coalescing cells; `DeclOverlayOwner` independent path-lock/`close_pending`/generation-ABA mutex (not the preserved high-water); `LazyTransport`/`LazyOverlayCore` establishment singleflight and `carrier_gates` (not the fenced 15/20/2-second durations); `ProjectTsserverProvider::providers` keyed `OnceCell`; `LazyManagedTypeProvider` activation cell/mutex/cooldown; the per-client cold-start validation join in `TsgoClient` and `ApiAttachClient`; the `project_bootstraps` reservation and its early-return callers; and the tsgo `ClientHandle::request` admission-before-select, post-dequeue deadline reset, and cancellation polling. Editor `typescriptPluginRefreshScheduler` and Native Preview `transition` are TCM4 deletions, not `ProviderHub`. G5 deletes provider pseudo-idle scheduling. K3 verifies every residual and deletes any missed residue before K3 can close; K3 separately owns deletion of the global notification/latch ladder after consumer cutover. Mapper-process JSON-RPC admission is TCM2, not H2; oracle-client snapshot flights consume G2 and are TCM3.

The two `verter_tsgo_api` cold-start joins convert TOGETHER, as one establishment operation, or neither does — they are one mechanism duplicated across two clients, and deleting one while the other lives leaves the class alive. What is deleted is the ad-hoc double-checked `AtomicBool` + async-`Mutex` join, not the rail it guards: `gate::require_integer_snapshot_handle` and its fail-closed flag-flips-only-after-full-decode ordering are preserved correctness, and a refused first call must still never unlock the fast path. `ApiAttachClient::leased_projects` is NOT part of this row — it is a per-tsconfig `openProjects` idempotency ledger whose lock is released before the request await by design, and H2 does not convert it into a join. That H2 reaches into `verter_tsgo_api` here is the same reach it already has over `ClientHandle::request`: `ProviderHub` being LSP-only bounds it away from EDITOR-side (`packages/`) cells, not away from provider mechanics in lower crates.

## Proof

Exact-generation tests force provider application, foreground promotion, supersession, full-queue cancellation/deadline on `ClientHandle::request` before admission becomes available, and the single permitted reopen retry. A test must fail if a sleep or idle signal substitutes for the applied receipt. Real-process shared-provider tests use protocol readiness, resource isolation, one independent real monotonic watchdog, and are serialized; H2 does not close while those tests remain contention-sensitive through nested in-test Cargo builds, unsynchronized shared-provider processes, or polling-discovered initialize frames. The shared-overlay 20-second fallback remains untouched without the required ruling.
