# H3 — Atomic readiness and stale-safe publication

**Status:** LOCKED — contains binding acceptance criteria from the ratified timing directive; this charter is not separately authorized or dispatched. See the [landed timing ruling](../rulings/ARCHITECT-RULING-2026-08-23-TIMING-ARCHITECTURE.md).  
**Class:** Foundational.  
**Predecessors:** `F1`, `H1`, `H2`.

## Objective

Settle foreground LSP demand against its exact basis immediately while retaining bounded latest-wins quiet windows only for background policy.

## Acceptance criteria

- Foreground hover, completion, navigation, references, rename, code actions, semantic tokens, inlay hints, and similar user requests do not wait for edit debounce.
- Foreground demand immediately joins or promotes the exact G2 producer flight.
- Background diagnostics/indexing may use a quiet window with latest-wins coalescing.
- Each background quiet-window domain has exactly one lifecycle owner.
- LSP debounce and edit-triggered import publication share one quiet-window policy and one lifecycle owner (`SyncCoordinator`).
- No detached sleeping task is spawned per edit.
- Cross-file edits causally invalidate exact dependency read sets.
- Replies and publications are stamped with their exact basis and discarded when superseded.
- A fast stale response is a correctness failure, never a latency success.
- Unrelated edits do not invalidate unrelated requests.

## Global readiness consumer cutover

H3 migrates LSP/client behavior away from `$/verter/ready` and `$/verter/typeProviderSyncComplete` to exact per-demand basis/receipt settlement. The cutover covers the `background_init` `scanner_done_tx`/`scanner_done_rx` and `ready_announced_tx`/`ready_announced_rx` latches, `await_scan_complete_after_ready`, `WorkspaceScannerConfig::done_tx`, and its empty-workspace, no-carrier-route, and normal-loop-completion send sites. It also covers four notification send sites across three components: the `TypeProviderSyncComplete` and `VerterReady` sends in `background_init`, the `TypeProviderSyncComplete` send in `sync_orchestration::notify_editor_carrier_store_changed`, and the `TypeProviderSyncComplete` send in `SyncCoordinator`; plus consumers/definitions in `packages/language-shared`, `packages/vue-vscode` (including E2E helpers), `packages/dx-harness`, and `packages/benchmark`. The incompatible meanings of `gen` are never translated into one final readiness value. If the scanner-done signal remains after the ladder is removed, H3 migrates it to scanner-owner-local, non-authoritative owned causal progress that cannot authorize provider or LSP readiness; otherwise H3 deletes it. K3 is the primary deletion owner for the old protocol types, notifications, readiness latches, four notification sends, and consumer wiring once this cutover is complete.

H3 is the primary deletion owner for its detached import/semantic timers, duplicate quiet-window path, wall-clock typing cooldown, capture-without-join behavior, displaced IDE-repair call paths, public `LspConfig` topology switches `suppress_edit_debounced_import_publication` and `suppress_sync_coordinator_signal`, and any hover-provenance producer/cache not migrated to the exact contract. Those two suppress flags are not timing architecture; they create a non-production topology and must not remain as production configuration. **They are already gone**: the deterministic-tests block removed both in `ec351a7f1`, and neither has a hit in `crates/` or `packages/` today, so this row is discharged rather than pending and H3 inherits no work from it. K3 verifies every residual and deletes any missed residue before K3 can close.
