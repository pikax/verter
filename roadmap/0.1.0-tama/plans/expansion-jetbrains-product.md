## Boundaries

- **JBT0 → JBT1.** JBT0 ratifies the falsifiable gate (JetBrainsBaselineManifest, RequiredWorkflowMatrix, SuperiorityPredicate methodology). JBT1 lands the pinned build skeleton and JVM gate runner; nothing in JBT0 pre-certifies adapter code, and no superiority claim is unlocked by a skeleton.
- **JBT0 → JBT1H.** The baseline manifest's `pending-capture` pins are JBT1H's to capture with the real-IDE harness; guessed pins are never admissible. JBT1H also owns the JBT0-AC-BASIS incremental-vs-fresh binding on the same receipt basis.
- **JBT9** is the only node that records measured parity outcomes against the RequiredWorkflowMatrix; a missing required workflow blocks promotion even when measured margins pass (JBT0-AC3).
- **JBT10** is the only node that records measured comparative margins under the predeclared methodology; a single selected microbenchmark cannot certify overall superiority (JBT0-AC2).
- **JBT11** evaluates the SuperiorityPredicate conjunction; the product terminal cannot declare superiority from installation, protocol smoke, or comparator runs with diagnostics disabled.
- **JBT3** owns coexistence: the official plugin and the Verter adapter never hold two active semantic authorities in one project.

## Rules inherited by every node in this train

- The comparator is official WebStorm in its recommended/default mode with diagnostics enabled; alternate service-powered modes are recorded rows, never weakened substitutes (JBT0-AC1).
- Every comparison run binds the DX0 ProductReceiptBasis; stale, wrong-handle and guessed-pin results are rejected.
- TypeScript stays the semantic-rail authority; inspection answers stay labelled; no second semantic/type/project engine inside the client.
- Measurement uses WSP's equal-work corpus with all required features enabled; unavailable metrics are labelled, never guessed; results obtained by doing less required work are void.
- Every operation this train exposes supplies its DX1 registration, executable adapter and replay case before promotion (JBT0-AC-EXPOSURE).
