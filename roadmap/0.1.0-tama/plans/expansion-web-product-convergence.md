## Boundaries

- **WDX0 → WDX1.** WDX0 ratifies the disposition matrix, evidence-class policy, historical-completions preservation and evidence-routing obligations. WDX1 turns them into executable scenario fixtures and routing; nothing in WDX0 pre-certifies WDX1 code.
- **WDX1 → WDX2.** Qualification (WDX2) consumes routed evidence from every portfolio terminal (fan-in pinned in the matrix: 38 terminals plus WDX1); it is the receiving amendment that turns "receiving amendments" into checked facts.
- **WDX2 → WDX3.** The acceptance terminal (WDX3) checks real completed artifacts — capability rows with runtime-observation receipts, DOC1-tested examples/reference, migration notes — never templates or screenshots.
- **WDX0 is not semantic execution.** Producing trains own their capability dimensions; this train owns scope routing, evidence classes and selected release claims only.

## Rules inherited by every node in this train

- One final owner per recommendation; two final owners or a proof-only item fails coverage review (WDX0-AC2).
- Static proof, runtime observation and estimates are never interchangeable; support claims require runtime observation from the item's own terminal (evidence-class policy).
- Historical completions and the vue/svelte advertised catalog keep their standing; the added portfolio starts with zero advertised surfaces.
- No new kernel, global web graph, status store or scheduler; the shared kernel stays sole owner; exposure obeys DX0's descriptor registration and HostExecutionClass law.
