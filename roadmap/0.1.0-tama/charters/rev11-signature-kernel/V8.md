## Independently acceptable outcome

The kernel is proven against the locked evidence and the legacy is gone: the signature corpus, the section 5.9 determinism matrix, the generated-artifact byte checks, and the real-project replay suites run green with zero unexplained mismatches; the semantic-difference ledger classifies every difference with causal proof for order-induced rows; latency, allocation, work, concurrency, and sustained-edit memory results are published against the registered budgets with every regression investigation resolved; and the retired names, producers, registries, skills, and charters are updated together. One independently acceptable outcome; no subblocks.
