# V7 — Runtime and lib Awaited over shared signature discovery

Readiness comes only from trusted implementation-ledger rows. A READY node may start; tooling does not validate commit locators, Git identity, receipts, leases, external state, or runtime admission.

Normative contract: `contracts/signature-kernel.md` sections 10.1 and 10.2. This block completes the D12 outcome's migration onto the kernel; D12's acceptance (D12-AC1..AC4) must keep holding.

## Independently acceptable outcome

The runtime thenability protocol reads the `then` member's signatures and the fulfillment callback's first parameter through `SignaturesOfType` and the shared positional model, keeping its state machine (non-thenable, adopted, malformed with defined recovery, incomplete) and recursion policy; lib `Awaited<T>` stays the ordinary conditional under `Instantiate` with the non-union assertion local to the distributed callback-arm step; the `Promise` fast path validates resolved declaration identity; and the Awaited-private readers are deleted. One independently acceptable outcome; no subblocks.

## Concrete surfaces and APIs

- `crates/verter_session/src/project_semantic_dispatch/build.rs` — `surface_thenability` (`:10624`) and `lib_awaited_surface` (`:11039`) obtain `then` signatures via `SignaturesOfType(then_member_type, Call, ctx)` and the callback's parameter via the positional accessor (receiver split, rest element read, nullish-arm exclusion, `any`/non-callable handling), preserving their documented divergence on optional/overloaded/`any` `then`; `awaited_call_signatures` (`:10698`), `first_parameter` (`:10812`), `non_nullish_arms` (`:10842`), and `runtime_nominal_call_signatures` (`:10766`, superseded by V3's population through `SignaturesOfType`) are deleted; `settled_non_thenable`'s `then`-by-name pre-filter is replaced by a surface-member presence check that carries no signature logic.
- `awaited_relation_output` (`:11382`) roots on the full dependency evidence of the reads it performed (then-member declaring files, augmentation fingerprints), not the operand alone.
- Receiver eligibility: an incompatible `this` on `then` is decided through the relation authority as the checker does; recursive thenables, constrained/open generics, and the `IntrinsicApplication(Awaited)` deferral are unchanged in meaning.
- Wrappers: `semantic_query/flow_return_result.rs` and `flow_return.rs::materialize_flow_return_wrap` keep their ownership; generator and async-generator canaries stay green.
- Mutation boundary: those surfaces and their test homes.
