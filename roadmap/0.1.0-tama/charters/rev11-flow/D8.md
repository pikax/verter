## Amendment: D8 is the convergence gate for the flow train's deferred rows (architect ruling, 2026-09-01)

Architect ruling, verbatim:

> **SEPARATE GOVERNANCE REPAIR; it does not block D2B re-certification.** One
> real DAG convergence node may cover the set: use D8, amend its charter with
> every deferred row and acceptance test, retarget dispositions to D8, and
> remove the false scheduling claim from `is_scheduled_block()`; per-owner nodes
> and a parallel debt ledger are unnecessary.

**What was measured.** Deferral dispositions across this train were gated on a
block's close — `U6.NARROW_LATTICE`, `U6.VALUE_INFERENCE`, `U6.CALL_RESOLVE`,
`U6.NARROW_INSTANCEOF`. None of those is a DAG node. `U6.NARROW_LATTICE` appears
zero times anywhere under `roadmap/0.1.0-tama/`; it exists only as a Rust enum
display label. The other three appear only in charter and decision prose.
`grep 'id = "U6' authority/dag/` returns nothing, and none has a charter. So
every "gated on that block's close" disposition was unenforceable by the trusted
ledger, while the corpus's own `Owner::is_scheduled_block()` reported all `U6*`
owners as scheduled — code asserting a schedule the DAG did not contain.

**Corrected contract.** D8 is the single real convergence gate for those
deferrals. The `U6.*` owner labels remain useful as a statement of WHICH
semantic authority owes the fix, but they are not schedulable units; the
schedulable obligation is D8's close. D8 may not close while any row below is
still in its deferred state.

### Deferred rows this node gates

Each row is pinned so retiring it is a deliberate re-pin rather than a silent
change; the pin depth and admission state DIFFER per row and are recorded per
class below. The subtype-reunion and unreduced-intersection rows are
`KnownOwed`, clean and warm, with full-depth value pins plus exact audit pins.
`N88` is `KnownOwed` but SHALLOW-pinned — its checker text is not yet
comparer-parseable, so its `SHALLOW_PINNED_ROWS` ledger entry is the tripwire —
and never warm behind its typed gap. `N90` and `N94` carry full-depth value and
audit pins but are `candidates: 0` and never warm; their typed gap is exactly
what their acceptance retires.

- **Subtype-reunion class** (`X26`, `X36`, `X39`, `X45`, `X46`, `X47`, `X48`,
  `X49`, `X55`) — the checker absorbs a subtype into its supertype on reunion;
  the substrate keeps every arm. Extensionally EQUAL to the checker, ratified
  as acceptable residue. Semantic authority: value inference.
  Acceptance: each row's published node equals the checker's, and its pins are
  retired in the same change.
- **`N88`** — `"k" in x` where no arm declares the key. Superset behind a typed
  `FlowGap`, never warm. Acceptance: publishes the checker's
  `Record<k, unknown>` intersection, or its gap is retired with a recorded
  reason.
- **`N90`** — `typeof x === "function"` over `object` where lib `Function` does
  not resolve. Superset behind a typed gap, never warm. Acceptance: resolves the
  global and publishes the checker's answer.
- **`N94`** — the `instanceof` direction a structural comparison cannot decide
  (downcast to a subclass, structural twin). Typed gap, `candidates: 0`.
  Acceptance: decided against the checker, or the gap is ratified permanent.
- **Unreduced intersection residue** (`N106`, `N115`) — the canonical layer
  publishes `{} & "x"` where the checker prints `"x"`. Extensionally EQUAL to
  the checker, so this is the same acceptable-residue class as the
  subtype-reunion rows, not a wrong answer. It follows that precedent rather
  than adding intersection reduction to the canonical layer, which the standing
  ruling forbids for the reunion twin. Semantic authority: value inference.
  Acceptance: the published node equals the checker's, and the pins are retired
  in the same change.
- **Conservative `instanceof` gap on a value that already matches** — `x: K |
  KSub` under `x instanceof K` publishes `K | KSub`, EQUAL to the checker, yet
  records `FlowGap(GuardNarrowing)` and refuses warm admission. A lost warm, not
  a wrong answer: the arm fails closed because a structural comparison cannot
  decide a downcast, and here the conservative answer coincides with the
  checker's. Pinned in-tree as
  `N138_instanceof_matching_union_subject_loses_warm_only` (full-depth value +
  audit pin; `MatchesChecker` with the typed gap and `candidates: 0` pinned),
  so retiring or regressing the conservative result is loud. Acceptance
  (architect ruling, verbatim): "Retire when complete-result admission proves
  the conservative `instanceof` result equals the checker result and suppresses
  `FlowGap(GuardNarrowing)` for `K | KSub`, while genuine downcasts such as N94
  remain gapped."
- **Checker-syntax comparer coverage** — 14 rows whose recorded checker text
  `checker_syntax::parse` cannot parse, so they can never be deep-pinned and
  compared: methods and accessors (`A15`, `X14`); `readonly` modifiers (`B03`,
  `B04`, `B10`, `CC03`); print elision, `... 37 more ...` (`E03`); optional
  members (`E04`, `H02`, `X10`, `X27`); generic arguments (`N88`, `X18`,
  `X19`). Array shorthand (`T[]`) is parsed, which removed `N74` and `X108`
  from this set. Acceptance: the comparer parses them, or each records the
  reason as `N88` already does.

Any further row deferred against a `U6.*` owner is added here in the same change
that defers it. A deferral that is not listed here has no gate.

`N96` and `N97` appeared briefly in this class and were then made to MATCH the
