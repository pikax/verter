Close the complete amended Rev11 architecture and compiler-bridge ancestry. L2 supplies equivalent-work and integrated memory evidence; SG0 supplies restored shipped-configuration behavioral execution. No new semantic, admission, storage or provider mechanism belongs in this terminal.

## Binding closure contract

Follow contracts/resource-and-finalization.md, SG0 and L4. This terminal owns the future architecture-closure report schema, executable completeness validator and negative fixtures defined there, plus the roadmap closure report, owning architecture/testing/contributor documentation and bounded residual deletions already assigned to this node. ## Acceptance and discriminating evidence

- **L4-AC1 — sole-owner outcome:** derive and schema-check the required outcome/consumer inventory against current Rev11 DAG ancestry, including compiler-bridge ancestors, charters, owning contracts and amendments. Every required owner other than L4 itself must have a dependency path to L4; reject an unknown or pathless owner. L4 owns only its declared terminal verification/documentation/deletion outputs. The executable closure validator requires the owner and every required transitive predecessor to have `status = "implemented"` in the trusted ledger, rejecting absent or pending rows even when L4 itself is implemented/COMPLETE. Map every outcome to its sole final owner, consumers, displaced-route disposition and actual evidence. Explicitly classify optional and historical rows; no intended outcome disappears.
- **L4-AC2 — positive contract:** validate a required per-train cumulative review population, including APPLICATION.md checkpoint coverage and resolved material findings, and an explicit public capability population including declared unsupported/partial cases. Omitted rows, stale or missing required review coverage, open deferrals, unresolved P0/P1 and undispositioned exceptions reject closure. Unsupported/partial behavior cannot stand in for a required complete outcome.
- **L4-AC3 — incremental equivalence:** all applicable required commands/jobs selected nonzero work and completed on the accepted candidate with matching prerequisites and zero unexpected skips. Every evidence row names its local-execution or CI owner and includes a complete terminal summary covering all selected/executed actions, required metrics and prerequisites. Require the complete controlling performance/memory metric and normal/pressure outcome populations under the closure contract; reject omitted, unmeasured or failing required cells. Include native/WASM/provider, map/encoding, specialized workload and restored shipped-cfg evidence; core PASS alone is insufficient. Memory/latency failures return to their owners rather than being repaired or waived in this terminal.
- **L4-AC4 — bounded work:** owning documentation describes the final system and the temporary CLAUDE.md program banner is removed only when accurate. Run the independent final train review covering this candidate and all implemented outcomes in addition to architecture-3.
