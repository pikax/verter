# SKT3 — SvelteKit first-class product convergence

## Independently acceptable outcome and owners

Certify and promote SvelteKit as a first-class project profile after complete applicable project, semantic, IDE, build, public-surface, performance, security, and deletion proof. SKT3 adds no semantics.

## Surfaces, APIs, and predecessor contracts

Expected surfaces are generated SvelteKit capability/conformance/deletion manifests, exact fixtures, docs, and terminal receipts. APIs: `SvelteKitCapabilitySnapshot`, `SvelteKitProductReceipt`, `SvelteKitLegacyDeletionManifest`. `SKT2` supplies implementation; `LSO10` supplies the converged current-product operation authority and capability truth consumed by the profile; `PER0` supplies cache/cancellation/zero-work/performance law.

## Binding architecture and subblocks

1. Freeze exact SvelteKit/Svelte/Vite/adapter epochs and required route/role/type/IDE/build/public cells.
2. Validate one candidate, delete manifest-listed legacy routes/readers/claims, and prove structural no-bypass.
3. Run incremental/fresh, config/generated/route transitions, cancellation, coexistence, secret-redaction/security, latency, work, handle, and RSS terminals.
4. Publish versioned evidence for genuinely inapplicable cells and the immutable product receipt. Any unsupported applicable required cell blocks promotion.

Terminal discoveries reopen SKT0–SKT2. Shared laws apply.

## Deletions, forbidden designs, acceptance, and verification

- **SKT3-AC1:** every applicable first-class cell has exact evidence and current capability truth.
- **SKT3-AC2:** planted legacy reader/route, secret leak, or manual claim is rejected.
- **SKT3-AC3:** full transition matrix equals fresh and clears stale publication.
- **SKT3-AC4:** bounded-work/latency/allocation/handle/RSS and disabled zero-work gates pass.
- Delete only manifest-listed displaced SvelteKit authority and migration flags; forbid terminal fixes or claiming parity from Svelte support alone.
- Abort and reopen the precise predecessor on any gap.
- Verify full SvelteKit matrix, canonical gate, strict DAG validation, and `architecture-3`.

PPR1 consumes this product alongside NUX3. Ceiling: 300 LOC, 3 files, 1 package; ledger presence records completion.
