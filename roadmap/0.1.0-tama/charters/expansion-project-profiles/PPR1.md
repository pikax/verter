# PPR1 — Paired Nuxt-SvelteKit first-class parity lock

## Independently acceptable outcome and owners

Lock the generic project-profile architecture only after both Nuxt and SvelteKit are independently promoted and adversarial cross-profile evidence proves the shared vocabulary is framework-neutral. PPR1 is a proof/convergence node and adds no semantics.

## Surfaces, APIs, and predecessor contracts

Expected surfaces are paired capability/counterfixture manifests, generic-schema guards, public capability projections, and the convergence receipt. APIs: `ProjectProfileConvergenceReceipt`, `ProjectProfileParityMatrix`, `ProjectProfileCapabilitySnapshot`. `NUX3` and `SKT3` supply independently accepted products; `COX0` supplies coexistence/capability publication law.

## Binding architecture and subblocks

1. Compare required cells by applicability, evidence quality, lifecycle modes, and public surfaces—not by forcing identical framework features.
2. Run Nuxt facts through SvelteKit negative fixtures and vice versa to reject shared-schema leakage and false activation.
3. Prove mixed monorepo, nested root, coexistence, disable/enable, version transition, and project open/close behavior.
4. Publish separate Nuxt/SvelteKit capability truth under one generic profile catalog.

Neither profile may depend on the other's implementation, fixtures, generated files, or route vocabulary. Shared laws apply.

## Deletions, forbidden designs, acceptance, and verification

Delete only generic migration flags/manual combined capability claims. Forbid adding profile semantics, lowering the weaker profile's required cells, calling counterfixtures first-class support, and selecting Next or another profile through this node.

- **PPR1-AC1:** both terminal receipts are exact/current and all generic required cells have two independent applicable proofs or explicit non-applicability.
- **PPR1-AC2:** planted Nuxt-shaped generic field, SvelteKit-shaped route assumption, or cross-activation fails.
- **PPR1-AC3:** mixed-profile transition sequences equal fresh and withdraw only affected capabilities.
- **PPR1-AC4:** inapplicable/disabled profiles perform zero capture/index/semantic/bundler work; churn plateaus.
- Performance evidence compares per-profile capture, contribution, index, LSP, and bundler work and retained snapshots across open/close, enable/disable, version transitions, and long mixed-project churn; parity cannot be inferred from wall time alone.
- Abort and reopen PPR0 or the precise profile predecessor on any semantic/schema gap.
- Verify paired full matrices, canonical gate, strict DAG validation, and `architecture-3`.

This is the project-profile convergence product. Public capability publication and any future project-profile train consume its separate Nuxt/SvelteKit catalog receipts; no future profile inherits either framework's semantics. Ceiling: 300 LOC, 3 files, 1 package; ledger presence records completion.
