## Surfaces, APIs, and predecessor contracts

Expected surfaces are generated Nuxt capability/conformance/deletion manifests, exact fixtures, docs, and terminal receipts. APIs: `NuxtCapabilitySnapshot`, `NuxtProductReceipt`, `NuxtLegacyDeletionManifest`. `NUX2` supplies implementation; `LSO10` supplies the converged current-product operation authority and capability truth consumed by the profile; `PER0` supplies cache/cancellation/zero-work/performance law.

## Binding architecture and subblocks

1. Freeze exact Nuxt/Nitro/tool epochs and required role/route/import/type/IDE/build/public cells.
2. Validate one candidate, delete manifest-listed legacy Nuxt routes/readers/claims, and prove structural no-bypass.
3. Run incremental/fresh, config/layer/generated transitions, cancellation, coexistence, security, latency, work, handle, and RSS terminals.
4. Publish versioned evidence for genuinely inapplicable cells and the immutable product receipt. Any unsupported applicable required cell blocks promotion.

Terminal discoveries reopen NUX0–NUX2. Shared laws apply.

- **NUX3-AC1:** every applicable first-class cell has exact evidence and current capability truth.

- **NUX3-AC3:** full transition matrix equals fresh and clears stale publication.

- Abort and reopen the precise predecessor on any gap.
- Verify full Nuxt matrix, canonical gate, strict DAG validation, and `architecture-3`.
