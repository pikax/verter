# Block sizing contract

A dispatchable leaf owns one independently acceptable authority, migration, or cutover. Target: at most 800 production LOC, 8 production files, and 2 related crates/packages. Mandatory architect rescope: over 1,500 production LOC, 12 files, 3 unrelated crates/packages, or public/wire, unsafe, concurrency, or lifetime work combined with another major concern. A dispatchable charter may not contain suggested independently acceptable subblocks. Convergence is at most 300 LOC/3 files, creates no authority, and performs only bounded validation, named residue deletion, or an atomic route switch.
