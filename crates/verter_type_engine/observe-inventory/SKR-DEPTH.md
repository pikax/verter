The `OPENNESS_NESTING` thread-local and its 256-level re-entry guard are removed: the explicit work stack replaces the native re-entry they bounded. No optional counter, trace or history is added; the depth tests read verdicts, published surfaces and the existing test-support dispatch counters, not visit or depth telemetry.
