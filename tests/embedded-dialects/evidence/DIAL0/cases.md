proof. Paths below identify current behavioral evidence and its limits.

Current style and map tests discriminate native admission, fabricated output
facts and map refusal. They do not prove a Pug parser, custom PostCSS grammar,
new format view, full variable resolution, or public manifest claim enforcement.
Those gaps remain explicit producer obligations. The constitution's negative
claim is falsified by any cell asserting full authored support with only
`format` or `compile` evidence; review the operation evidence, not a package's
upstream feature list.

Concern: correctness. Prevents: plausible false-positive support and unsafe
source edits when output exists without authored facts. Not covered because:
current transform/parser tests cannot establish future VIM/DX projection or
cross-host operation support. These cases implement the charter's AC2/AC4
obligations, not optional new hardening.
