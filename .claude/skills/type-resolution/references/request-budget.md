**Cost receipts: budgets are warmth-independent.** Every stored semantic result carries the `DemandCostReceipt` of the computation that produced it (`project_semantic_dispatch/cost_receipt.rs`): its exclusive work units, construction bytes and request operations, plus the receipts it consumed. The memo cannot admit a candidate without one (`ProducerLease::settle` takes the sealed receipt; a build without one is never admissible), and every read envelope carries it (`CacheRead::receipt`, a `ReadReceipt::Priced` for any stored or computed result; only recursion carriers, cancellations and typed refusals are `Unpriced`) — warm hits, subscribers to another task's flight, the modeless relation / flow-return / call-resolution reads, the prefix peek, backfilled prefixes (each carrying the receipt of the walk hops that reached it, sealed as the walk reaches the prefix — never the whole walk's) and SCC members (which share the component root's receipt). A receipt also carries its operation footprint — the query depth, instantiation height and drive requirement, and tail-step peak its cold run needed. The dispatch replays it (`ConnectedDemandLedger::replay_admit`) before the result serves: the unpaid part of its closure is charged to the connected demand (work, bytes, depth) and to the request's projection fuse (operations, paid once per request), or — when the demand cannot pay — the stored candidate stays for any demand that can and this entry computes the result itself (`ClaimAttempt::recompute`), tripping exactly where a cold evaluation would. Replay also refuses a receipt whose footprint the reader's allowances (query depth, instantiation budget at the reader's frame depth, tail budget) could not complete; construction bytes are charged on replay but never refuse it. A recomputing claim produces on a flight of its own, and a subscriber woken on another producer's incomplete, unpriced result produces alone too, so neither stops where somebody else's demand did. A claim the memo cannot serve back to the dispatcher's own view (the candidate records facts newer than the view) reads the result the dispatcher's own build published instead of computing it again (`published_results`), so a cold request computes each result once and is charged exactly as its warm repeat; a result that was never admitted is recomputed — and charged — in both. A cold build opens a cost scope (`ConnectedDemandLedger::record_cost`) that every charge accrues to; an incomplete build abandons it into its consumer's. The deferred evaluator's hash-cons memo keeps the same receipts. An isolated root that runs out of its allowance seals its refusal (`semantic_query_memo/refusal_summary.rs`) under its exact identity — the canonical query, the interned `BudgetProfile` of every allowance it answers to, and the request state it entered at — and an exact repeat answers from it without evaluating, after revalidating the facts the failed evaluation observed. A demand trip on work or depth and an operation's own allowance refusal (`OPERATION_BUDGET`) seal; cancellation, supersession, torn views, faults and the soft construction-byte rail never do. A root seals only when nothing else spent in its request while it ran (`RequestBudget::spent_alone_since` against the ledger's own `request_effects`), and a repeat checks the request state and applies the refusal's request effects in one atomic step (`RequestBudget::apply_refusal`). Profiles intern weakly and are reclaimed with their last holder; receipts and summaries are charged to the process retention account, and a summary the account declines is returned, not sealed.

**Primary defense + fuse backstop.** The L1 carrier-stop is the PRIMARY defense for the open-generic class at every entrance: a finite-large legitimate published surface decidable as CLOSED terminates on its own merits and is published. The per-request projection budget (`request_budget.rs`) is an ARMED-by-default runaway fuse (`projection_op_budget == 0` ⇒ effective cap 2000); the projection keys plus `Instantiate`/`Conditional` count toward it; an armed fuse that trips returns `BudgetExceeded` as a genuine partial (refused warm admission — the no-poison invariant).

**No SILENT semantic-complexity caps in the semantic layer.** A hard-coded step/depth cap used as a SEMANTIC budget — one that silently truncates a resolution and hands back a bare node a consumer classifies as settled — is forbidden. Three legacy silent caps were deleted or reclassified:

- `evaluate_deferred_semantic_node`'s `for _ in 0..32` — DELETED; replaced by stack-local `EvaluationGuard` cycle detection.
- `PathWalker::max_depth = 64` — DELETED; replaced by `WalkGuard` cycle detection.
- Parser-level `MAX_TYPE_RESOLUTION_DEPTH = 64` — RENAMED to `PARSER_SYNTACTIC_DEPTH_LIMIT = 256` and documented as syntactic stack-safety, not a semantic budget.

**Retained runaway-safety fuses (distinct from — not a contradiction of — the above).** Two hard fuses REMAIN and are correct precisely because visited-node cycle detection cannot bound FRESH-NODE regrowth (each operator re-dispatch / `Instantiate` step can mint a brand-new node id the `visited` set has never seen, so a cycle set alone does not terminate a runaway): the deferred evaluator's recursion ceiling `EVALUATE_DEFERRED_DEPTH_CEILING = 256` (stack safety) and the residual-carrier demand step fuse `STRUCTURAL_FACT_DEMAND_FUSE = 64` (backing `normalize_node_for_structural_fact_demand` / `peel_node_for_uninstantiated_carrier_fact_demand` and `resolve_signature_source_carrier`). Neither is a silent semantic cap: a trip returns a TYPED `ResultCompleteness::Partial` (`DEFERRED_EVALUATION_LIMIT` / `STRUCTURAL_FACT_DEMAND_LIMIT`) — node-HIDING at the demand surface (`StructuralFactDemandOutcome::Partial` carries no `SemanticNodeId`), never warm-admitted (`ComputeAdmission::ReturnOnly`), and folded into the enclosing build's taint frame (`into_active_query_build_node` → `fold_local_partial_completeness`) so no consumer can ever classify a truncated carrier as a settled result.

**Bounded-loop annotation convention.** Any bounded loop in `crates/verter_type_engine/src/project_semantic_dispatch/` MUST be annotated `// bounded-loop: <reason>` on the preceding line. Approved reasons: `fence-retry` (CLAUDE.md completion-fence 3-retry rule); and a runaway-safety fuse / `visited`-set cycle stop that returns a TYPED `Partial` and is never warm-admitted (the residual-carrier step fuse in the structural-fact demand loop and `resolve_signature_source_carrier`, plus the annotated `visited`/fuse loops in `build.rs` / `relation.rs` / `absorb.rs` / `walk.rs`). A bare numeric cap with no typed-`Partial` exit is NOT an approved reason.
